IReply Platform.

01 // PROJECT SUMMARY

A private hiring workflow

Built as an internal recruitment platform for IReply, this project centralizes job posting and applicant tracking in a self-hosted environment. It connects HR workflows with the company website and Google Jobs while keeping candidate data on the company’s own servers.

02 // PROJECT DEEP DIVE

From job post to candidate status

The platform gives HR a single place to create roles, manage applicants, edit listings, and move candidates through the hiring pipeline. Live syncing publishes approved jobs to the company website, while structured data helps listings appear in Google Jobs without exposing candidate information outside the organization.
